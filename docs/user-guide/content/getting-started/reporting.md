# Generating and viewing reports

## What are reports?

{{< img src="images/cc-reports.png" alt="Reports" >}}

Content Controller’s built-in reporting tools make it easy to gain insight into course usage and learner performance to make strategic decisions. To access Reports, click the **Reports** button in the top right of your screen.

You can view the following reports in Content Controller:

* [Usage](../reports/usage-reports.md)
* [Course](../reports/course-reports.md)
* [Learner](../reports/learner-reports.md)
* [Shared](../reports/shared-reports.md)
