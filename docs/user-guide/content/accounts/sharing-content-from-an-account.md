# Sharing content from an account

{{< img src="images/account-share-content.png" alt="Sharing content from an account" >}}

## How do I share or export a course from an account?

1. Navigate to the Account.
2. Select the **Content** tab.
3. Select the course you wish to share.
4. You can click configure to make changes to the player and window settings to apply to just the chosen account.
5. Click **Share** in the _Course Detail_ pane.
6. Select the specification that the Account LMS requires from the drop-down.
   {{< hint info >}}
   Content Controller can create sharable files in the following standards: SCORM 1.2, SCORM 2004 3rd and 4th Editions, AICC, cmi5, and LTI 1.1 and LTI 1.3.
   {{< /hint >}}
7. Click **Get Link** to create a download link.\
   You can click **Copy Link** to copy the download link to the clipboard to share with your customer. You can also immediately download the course .zip by clicking **Download Package.**

This exported package can now be uploaded into any third-party LMS that supports the specification you selected.

## How do I share multiple courses with an LMS?

{{< img src="images/account-share-multiple-content.png" alt="Sharing multiple courses from an account" >}}

You can also create a Bulk Share by selecting the checkbox next to the courses you'd like to include, click **Share** in the upper right of the screen, and follow the download process above.
